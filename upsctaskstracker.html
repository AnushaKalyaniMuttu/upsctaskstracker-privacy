UPSC Tasks Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

UPSC Tasks Tracker ("the app", package com.upsctasktracker.app) is developed and published by Anusha Kalyani Muttu. Contact: anushakalyanimuttu@gmail.com

Summary

The app helps you work through Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases, current-affairs articles, NCERT books and the Constitution of India, and keep your own notes. Your progress and notes are stored on your device. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send your data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

  • Progress: which days and articles you have marked as read, completed or starred, and your progress in NCERT books and the Constitution tracker.
  • Notes and highlights: your notes (per day, per article, and for The Hindu), highlights, and words you added from the dictionary.
  • Downloaded content: article text, so it can be read again and highlights stay in place, and downloaded NCERT PDFs.
  • Preferences: for example, the backup folder you chose and whether you dismissed the backup reminder.

This data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Internet: to fetch the content described below.
  • Notifications: to show NCERT download progress. You can decline or turn this off in Android settings.

Network requests and third-party sites

The app connects to the internet to fetch content. Like any web request, these sites can see your IP address and basic technical details such as your device's user agent. The sites are:

  • PIB releases and archive: pib.gov.in, archive.pib.gov.in
  • Current affairs and NCERT book lists and PDFs: drishtiias.com
  • Constitution articles: constitutionofindia.net
  • Word definitions: api.dictionaryapi.dev and en.wiktionary.org
  • The Hindu e-paper (optional): epaper.thehindu.com
  • ChatGPT (optional): chatgpt.com and related OpenAI sign-in pages

When you look up a word, that word is sent to the dictionary services above. The developer does not control these sites; each is covered by its own privacy policy.

Sign-in pages shown inside the app (The Hindu and ChatGPT)

The Hindu e-paper and ChatGPT open in an in-app browser. You sign in on each site's own page, and the app never sees or stores your password. The in-app browser keeps those sites' cookies on your device so you stay signed in. In the ChatGPT window, Sign out removes the chatgpt.com and openai.com cookies and site data that the in-app browser holds.

If you choose ChatGPT from the text-selection menu, the text you selected, wrapped in a short question, is copied to your clipboard and sent to ChatGPT in that window. Do not select text you do not want to send. What ChatGPT does with it is governed by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. Text you add to notes from a ChatGPT answer is saved on your device and labelled as coming from ChatGPT.

Backups

  • Back up to a file: you choose where the backup file is saved using Android's file picker. If you pick a cloud provider such as Google Drive, that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to the file.
  • Auto backup: if you turn it on, the app writes one backup file per day into a folder you choose and keeps only the newest few. No account or sign-in is involved; the app only writes files to that folder.
  • Backup files contain your notes, highlights and progress. They are not encrypted by the app, so keep them somewhere you trust. The developer has no access to these files.
  • The app allows Android's own system backup, so your device or Google account settings may also back up app data. That is controlled by your device settings, not by the developer.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share your data with third parties. The only transfers are the requests to the sites listed above and any backup file you choose to save yourself.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can delete notes, highlights and progress inside the app. Uninstalling does not delete backup files you saved elsewhere; you must delete those files yourself. Use Sign out in the ChatGPT window, or clear the app's data in Android settings, to remove in-app browser cookies.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
